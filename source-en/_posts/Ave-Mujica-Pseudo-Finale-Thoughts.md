So many emotions. Although the plot fell apart in places, the final two episodes delivered so extraordinarily well.

A lingering wave of nostalgia and regret washes over me:

*[It will be hard to ever again encounter a work like MyGO and Mujica, with its passionate fan creations, deep analyses, wild theories, and fervent discussions. Only regret remains: "Never again did I taste such good beans as that night, nor did I ever see such a good opera as that night."]*

Just as I was thinking this, on the verge of making peace with it all —

There is actually a sequel announced! I screamed out loud on the spot.

Hands clutched over my pounding heart amid breathless excitement, I only wanted to quiet down and thank the heavens.
