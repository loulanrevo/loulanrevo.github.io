Midterm agony biweek.

God, are you kidding me? Isn't my primary discipline Computer Science? Why am I in the Automation department taking Signals and Systems? It is genuinely brutal.

Learning Rust that my home department doesn't teach, Signals and Systems that the CS college doesn't teach, and Machine Learning that Telecommunications doesn't teach. The humorously chaotic curriculum gives me the identity crisis of an academic mutt. At least I laughed at my own frustration.

*But I'm a creep, I'm a weirdo  
What the hell am I doing here?  
What am I even still doing here?  
I don't belong here.  
I don't belong here at all.*
