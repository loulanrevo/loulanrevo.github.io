*Miracle Workers* live.

A live performance that truly delivers on its promises.

Pure sincerity, completely shedding the detached irony and endless deconstruction of the postmodern era.

Freedom, dignity, and love.

You can shout without reservation here.

Math rock with a touch of emo and post-punk self-reflection.

The studio recordings are remarkably restrained, but live on stage, it becomes an entirely different beast.

Truly an awe-inspiring experience.
