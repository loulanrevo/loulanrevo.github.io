*Le jiu shi* Chongqing, the Fog City.

In autumn and winter, it's either pouring rain or blanketed in fog.

How do you like an average of 104 foggy days a year?

Because of relentless rain, our mandatory physical fitness tests were pushed back four weeks in a row.

For an entire month, the sun was practically a myth.

A kid who grew up in coastal Xiamen simply cannot adapt to this.

Deprived of sunlight, serotonin levels plummet.

A daily relapse of unexplainable low spirits,  
wrapped purely in the chilly, desolate melancholy of winter.

Summon Master Hina, quick —  
Desperately need a 100% sunshine girl.

Abundant sunlight is an absolutely vital necessity of life.
