Found out where my best friend from elementary school ended up.

He moved back to his hometown for middle school. Starting from seventh grade, we kept in touch every summer break, but he remains the only elementary school classmate I still talk to.

Even though I knew he chose the humanities track and had barely any free time, I was still stunned to learn he got into Renmin University — couldn't help but feel deeply moved.

And the moment I scrolled through his Qzone feed, all the memories came flooding back at once.

Going to his house to play video games together; walking out the school gate to pick up his little sister, then for fun taking that bush-covered path barely two feet wide to walk home together, occasionally getting teased by those obnoxious twin brats from our class along the way.

Entering a speech recitation contest together, which was the first time I learned he suffered from hypoglycemia.

Borrowing a classmate's copy of *The Life of Hitler* together and being profoundly shaken; drawing stacks of homemade booklets on politics and history, so captivated by the words that I couldn't stop writing, ending up with my sections being entirely dialogue.

Being very close with my desk-mate, to the point our homeroom teacher suspected us of "puppy love." I forget why he was there too, but anyway the three of us squatted in the hallway during lunch break while the teacher talked to us with a gentle smile.

Later, he told me that when my desk-mate first transferred to our school in fourth grade, her smile was so radiant and luminous — he had had a crush on her ever since, his eyes full of yearning nostalgia as he recalled it.

Yet at the time, I didn't care about any of that at all; I just thought hanging out with her was fun. I even felt that spending time with her cut into hanging out with my other good buddies. Looking back now, it seems she really did get jealous once when I was chatting with other girls.

Right at that moment, I suddenly remembered: back in elementary school, I was so confident and outgoing. Not just that — so many things have changed since, and there are parts of myself today that I truly dislike. What on earth happened over the years?

I don't know how this line just popped into my head:

*"Hey, what kind of person do you want to become when you grow up?"*  
*"I'll answer that with the rest of my life!"*
