*My company employs me, therefore I am.*

Conveniently, watching the tournament came with a travel allowance, so I took the opportunity to visit Shanghai.

Learned so much yesterday at IQC.

Met lots of folks from our research group: Yu-Gi-Oh, MG, Class Rep, and Teacher Mao.

Two Grandmasters who had already been admitted to US master's programs shared pure, unadulterated gold.

Who would have guessed Teacher Mao had eight years of test-prep tutoring experience. His storytelling and way of illustrating principles immediately reminded me of my friend H.

Snapped group photos with the mentors.

Teacher K still remembered my voice — I had booked two one-on-one slots with him before.

Social anxiety made mingling nearly impossible; it was exhausting. Total physical depletion — when we got back, we crashed for three hours before waking up.

Hope I can hit Master level next year, have more solid footing, and return in style.

*"That year at nineteen, IQC gold level, standing there like a mere nobody...*  
*Back then, through tears I swore to you all: one day, you will see me."*
