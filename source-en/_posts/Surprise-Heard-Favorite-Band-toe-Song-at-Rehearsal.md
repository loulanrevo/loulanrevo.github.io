Walking past the sports field, I suddenly caught the opening notes of *Goodbye*.

From vaguely familiar to instantly dizzy with pure surprise.

In a melody only we know.

That sense of belonging and kinship — tears almost welled up.

The finest thing about this track is the drumming: the drummer pouring his whole heart in, proudly riding a tidal surge of rhythm.

*"When a drummer enters their flow state"* — live is truly the ultimate.
