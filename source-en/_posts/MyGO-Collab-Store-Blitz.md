Visited the Lawson Lawson x MyGO!!!!! x Ave Mujica collaboration store.

I had assumed that at this hour, all event quota tickets would have been long gone, and only stopped by to take a look.

When the clerk answered that slots were still available, several people around me exclaimed in unison: "Wait, there's still spots left?!"

The synchronization rate was hilarious.

Right away, someone brought up news of the Shanghai collab store being forced to shut down due to queueing and scalper disputes, plus the fridge magnet stamp campaign clearing out on day one.

In a place like this, the atmosphere among fellow fans was wonderfully fun.

I actually knew all about this — on the first day of the stamp rally, I only remembered late at night, checked the official site to see only 100+ left out of 4,000, and bolted straight to the Lawson outside our campus gate. Getting it was pure euphoria.

Felt like dopamine was gushing straight out of my skull — absolute peak joy!
