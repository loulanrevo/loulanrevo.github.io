The burning thirst for victory — who among them does not bear it?

Triple Crown dreams blossom; Triple Crown dreams shatter.

Yet the Goddess of Victory smiles upon only one.

One horse breaks away; all others fade into the dust.

How does one answer expectations? How does one honor the grueling toil?

A path of redemption; a pledge fulfilled; the supreme champion emerges.

Regret and unwillingness — how does one grow under the blunt battering of reality?

A story aimed straight at chasing the summit.

Also, Cygames taking direct control of the production makes the visual quality truly stellar.

Line art, storyboards, lighting, the raw surge of momentum and speed.
