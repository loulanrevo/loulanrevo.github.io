Running a high fever last night felt like being utterly possessed.

Maybe because I’d been reading *Les Misérables* before bed, I ended up dreaming about the French Revolution (never mind that *Les Misérables* isn't even about 1789).

Driven to the brink by crushing taxes, we sent King Louis straight to the guillotine together.

Then I followed Napoleon from Toulon across the Alps, through Egypt, all the way to the 18th of Brumaire.

I woke up just as we reached the Arc de Triomphe. Still completely out of it, I lay in bed trying desperately to cheer for Napoleon's coronation. After trying for over ten minutes, my throat went dry and I wanted some water. Sitting on the chair sipping water, I stared into space, wondering what on earth I had just been doing.

Back in bed, sleep still eluded me. My head kept racing with thoughts of the anti-French coalitions — my own inner voice was almost drowned out, crowded by moving artillery batteries and flanking cavalry. It wasn't until I realized I could still speak, could still act on my own thoughts, and that I was still myself — that I could finally drift off in peace.
