**Wilderness Cooperative**

Through their instruments, someone has let out a cry.  
A cry — and I need only follow, straight to the soul.  
The soul, shaking off its shackles for a while: *let me free, even if only tonight.*  
Tonight is about this era, about choices, about lives; they are revolutionaries of living, and I get a share too, of their free, fearless, burning heart.  
In my heart, a conviction suddenly wells up: I want to scatter my gratitude everywhere, and begin a brand-new life.
