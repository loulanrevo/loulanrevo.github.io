Drank some gin.

It kept coming up when reading *1984* — the name alone had always piqued my curiosity.

Pungent, invasive, almost unbearable; you could only gulp it down quickly like bitter medicine.

At forty-three percent ABV, the burning heat tracks every moment of where it is, face flushing hot, eyes almost welling with tears.

The stinging sensation quickly subsided, and suddenly the world felt a little lighter, a little more pleasant. Gradually, the aroma unfolded — musky, sharp, and refreshing, yet with an alcoholic intensity that almost made you wonder if it was disinfectant ethanol.

High spirits surged, but in an instant, that devastatingly impactful ending of *1984* came to mind, savoring it over and over.

In the end, accompanied by gin, I watched the film adaptation of *1984*.

Happy National Day. (laughs)
