**TL;DR:** MAN WANTED — Quantitative Finance. Join a research group formed by people I know. Substantial income.

To put it more formally: I am currently developing quantitative trading strategies for WorldQuant as an **independent consultant**.

How does a CS major like me end up diving into quantitative finance?  
Quite simply: because the compensation is genuinely generous.

You might be thinking: *Oh, it's just an ad, pass, not interested.*  
But I truly believe this is worth your while.  
I hope you take a moment to read through and give it serious thought.

*You should not throw away your shot.*

As long as you're curious, you're welcome to jump in, see what we do every day, and see what the income looks like.  
The more you understand, the better equipped you'll be to make an informed choice.

I started completely from scratch here with zero prior background; as long as you're willing to put in the work, it's totally achievable.

### Perks of Joining
- Learn Python, plus guided research paper reading sessions on how to go from reading an academic paper to reproducing its models.
- Monthly global online briefings; biweekly 15-minute one-on-one slots with mentors (e.g., mentors holding undergraduate degrees from top domestic finance universities and fully funded Cambridge master's degrees).
- Most importantly, the newly introduced **PPAC** system: even if you have zero initial ideas and just want to run code templates daily to collect the baseline stipend, that's already around 5k RMB pre-tax annually just for showing up.
- In the past, I might have said this has great potential with daily base payments reaching up to $120. Now my stance is simply: stop hesitating, come claim your slice of the pie.
- If you're willing to ramp up quantity and quality, the returns are far higher than that.

On the community forums, I saw a fellow student who joined in October 2024; in Q1 2025 alone, their quarterly bonus was over $8,000 USD.  
Deeply inspiring, and it quietly made me feel: *maybe I can pull that off too.*

Yes, **WorldQuant** operates on this exact crowdsourced strategy — one-of-a-kind in the industry:  
*Three commoners together outwit the master strategist.*

Their database is simply too vast. Hiring full-time on-site quants in the US to scour all of it is prohibitively expensive, and any single person's bandwidth is strictly limited.  
Instead, by lowering the barrier to entry and distributing the work to remote consultants worldwide, they scale massively.  
Thanks to currency exchange rates, compensation that might seem trivial to a Wall Street analyst is a very substantial sum for a college student in China.

In reality, most of the time is spent learning and brainstorming ideas; actual active working time rarely exceeds one hour a day.  
It's entirely remote and online. If you want income, submit alphas; if you take a break, there is zero pressure.

The feedback loop for monetizing knowledge is astonishingly fast: fresh insights and ideas translate almost immediately into new alpha submissions.  
I have to admit I stumbled through countless pitfalls along the way: cloud servers, Linux, Python environments, HTTP requests, and foundational financial concepts.  
Yet every day, submitting alphas generates income. Solving problems equals immediate reward.

*Effort yielding direct return* — in an era rife with aimless rat races and anxiety, such a positive feedback loop is an invaluable treasure.

*Teacher, I want to progress so badly.*

I've stepped into a completely new headspace: energized, diligent, and clear-minded.  
Because hard work actually pays off — and it pays quickly and well.  
At the very least, I feel what I do hardly justifies that kind of money.

Every morning when Eastern Time enters a new trading day, you get to see the income credited from today's alpha submissions.  
It's addictive. Seriously addictive.  
Now every day includes an extra budget of time purely for daydreaming.

I sincerely invite you to join our research team.  
Don't be shy — it's not some massive bureaucracy, just people in my circle.  
We already have two signed consultants, and the rest have submitted their initial reviews.

Because of non-compete agreements, you rarely find hardcore finance pros here.  
Everything I know was learned from the ground up, and across the broader platform, many users are older hobbyists who get dizzy just looking at raw code.  
Yet this isn't free money either. You know that in China, *High Return – Low Difficulty – Low Competition* is an impossible trinity.  
Still, the platform has only been active for two years, with around 8,000 consultants worldwide and merely ~400 active consultants in the China region. Learning about this opportunity felt like incredible luck.

For me, helping others is meaningful too. Last quarter, because I missed crucial information and lacked guidance, I left $30 on the table.  
*Studying in isolation without companions leaves one narrow and uninformed.*

I've already stepped on all the rakes and smoothed out the learning curve so you don't have to struggle alone.  
Naturally, if you join through my referral, the platform pays a referral bonus — think of it as my tuition fee for lecturing and mentoring whenever you need help.

As long as you're curious, come on in, check out our day-to-day workflow, and see what the earnings look like.  
The more you know, the better your choices will be.

Tomorrow the China region is hosting the June briefing.  
June 9th is the global consultant briefing (a poignant date indeed).  
You can join in to observe and ask me questions live on our voice channel!

Centered around earning together, we want to build a tight-knit study community.  
You are more than welcome to join.  
We need more friends and mutual support.
