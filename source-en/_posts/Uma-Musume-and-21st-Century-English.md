Saw on Bilibili that the *21st Century English* newspaper had a feature on *Uma Musume*.

What a delightfully unexpected crossover!

I immediately bolted to the school reading room to track it down.

By the time I arrived, they had already swapped out the display for the latest issue.

Feeling terribly apologetic, I interrupted the reading room librarian during her lunch break to help me dig through the archives.

The moment I mentioned that specific issue held special sentimental value for me, she gladly agreed right away.

The art of cooperation is simply having good manners!

Yet after rummaging high and low, we came up empty-handed.

The librarian suddenly recalled that another girl had also come looking for that exact same issue earlier. And since every other paper was still accounted for, someone must have slipped it away.

Uma fans are truly something else — I only wanted to snap a quick photo for the memories.

The librarian kept apologizing, then looked at the phone tucked in my pocket and suggested: "Why don't you scan the QR code on another issue to find it?"

I nodded eagerly and said sure, I'd scan it once I got home over the weekend.

Though I ended up buying the digital edition back home, it just didn't feel real anymore.

Still, today — April 16th — *Uma Musume: Road to the Top* officially premieres!

The legendary 1999 Classic Triple Crown — a once-in-a-lifetime trial, the shared dream of every contender.

Satsuki Sho, Japanese Derby, Kikuka Sho.

T.M. Opera O, Admire Vega, Narita Top Road.

Promises, burning passions, unyielding pride, the path of sovereigns — airing grandly on the very day of the Satsuki Sho!
