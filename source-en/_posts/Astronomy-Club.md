Skipped evening self-study to take photos of the moon at the astronomy club.

Because of relative motion, while the moon looks motionless to the naked eye, the telescope actually needs recalibrating every five or six minutes.

Brought to mind the law of quantitative change leading to qualitative change.

Listened to upperclassmen chat about the recent comet, Saturn's rings currently facing us edge-on, and things like that.

Actually, back when I first got into sci-fi and started playing *Stellaris*, I looked into astronomy a bit. In short, whether academic or purely as a hobby, it is exceptionally demanding on the individual.

Recalling this, I can't shake the feeling that university astronomy club might be the last time in my life I ever engage with astronomy-related things.

All the more reason it felt like the right choice. Starting tomorrow, I need to take it a step further: dare to skip useless classes and stop squandering time on the meaningless!
