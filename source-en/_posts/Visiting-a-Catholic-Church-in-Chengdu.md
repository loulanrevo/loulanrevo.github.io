Yesterday the church was holding a retreat (non-parishioners weren't admitted), so I missed out on attending an English Mass.

So this morning I got up bright and early to attend the very first Mass.

And being Lent today, I observed both fasting and abstinence, skipping breakfast entirely (breakfast literally meaning to "break the fast").

Right now, I am practically the truest embodiment of this Jubilee Year's theme: "Pilgrims of Hope."

But really, the biggest reason I came here is that this is the Cathedral of Chengdu — the only place in Sichuan and Chongqing, perhaps even the entire Southwest, that has Luce.

I originally thought I'd have to buy one, but the volunteer simply handed me a whole handful, completely for free.

Utter bliss — happy lucky smile yeah!

Luce is so cute! The Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception in Chengdu is full of kind souls. Wishing our enlightened Pope Francis everlasting health and triumph.

Amen.
